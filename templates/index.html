Stat Explanations

  • Rank: The power ranking position based on the PR Score (lower is better).
  • Team Name: The name of the fantasy football team.
  • Record: The team's current win-loss-tie record (e.g., 5-2-0).
  • Overall Wins: Wins if the team faced every other team (except itself) every week. Higher is better.
  • Consistency: A score measuring how consistent the team's scoring is, calculated as ((PPG - 3 * Standard Deviation) / (0.75 * League Average PPG)). Lower rank is better.
  • PPG: Points Per Game, the average points scored per matchup. Higher is better.
  • ROS Strength: Rest-of-Season roster strength ranking from FantasyPros (lower rank is better). Historical weeks may show N/A when point-in-time ROS data is unavailable.
  • PR Score: The composite power ranking score, calculated by ranking each stat and weighting them (lower is better).
